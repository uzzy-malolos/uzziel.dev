Bakunawa swallowed six of the seven moons. Wren, Mayari's apprentice, walks the islands on eclipse nights to win them back. That is the whole pitch, and the game is built to be played one-handed on a phone in the few minutes you have.

It is a survivors-like: you move, the weapons fire on their own, and every level-up offers a three-card draft. Island 1, Bamboo Village, is fully playable from the first night to the Tikbalang King. Lantern shrines, chests, weapon evolutions and a Moon Shrine for permanent upgrades round out the loop.

I wrote it in TypeScript on a 60 Hz fixed-step Canvas 2D loop with a DOM overlay for menus, so the game logic stays deterministic and the UI stays crisp. It ships as a PWA on the web and as a Capacitor Android build. Phones get dedicated landscape layouts for every screen, and saves can be exported and imported.
