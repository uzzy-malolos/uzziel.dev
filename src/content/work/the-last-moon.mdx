The Last Moon runs as a fast portrait or landscape survivors-like session built for both thumb drag on phones and keyboard or gamepad on desktop. Auto-firing folklore weapons fill the screen while players weave around creatures, dash out of danger with an invulnerability frame, and gather moon dust to draft upgrades or union evolutions.

## Five islands and four playable heroes

Each island brings its own creature mix, terrain feel, hazards, and three-phase boss encounter. Defeating a boss requires breaking its guard and stealing three trophies under pressure: golden hairs from the Tikbalang King in Bamboo Village, salted legs beneath the Manananggal Mother in Haunted Forest, three bells against the Queen of the Sirena in Drowned Shrine, Ember Shore's Smoking Kapre, and Mangrove Maze's Bungisngis, plus the hidden Biringan night that unlocks the unique Kubing instrument.

Across runs, players earn moon shards to spend at the Moon Shrine across 19 permanent blessings - from early survival picks like Woven Charm to high-tier revives like Mayari's Mercy and Twin Moons. Weapon evolutions fuse max-level gear into union forms such as Eclipse Edge, Kidlat Lash, Agua Bendita, Kulintang Chorus, and Mayari's Full Moon.

## Shipped editions and audio-visual pass

I wrote the engine in TypeScript on HTML5 Canvas 2D with zero runtime dependencies in the core loop, layered with an original adaptive score synthesized from kulintang, agung, kudyapi, and drums that swells as waves tighten. Recent passes added terrain footstep sounds, destructible jars and sacks, wet shore ripples, weapon gesture sprites, achieve-gated unlocks, and the 3D Lore Preview motion film.

The Free edition runs directly in the browser and on Android (Islands 1 and 2). The full Deluxe edition - unlocking Islands 3 through 5, all four heroes, and Moon Shrine depth - is live on [itch.io](https://uzzydev.itch.io/the-last-moon) for Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and Web, with a live browser build at [the-last-moon-omega.vercel.app](https://the-last-moon-omega.vercel.app).
