Jelly Rune Rush started as Color Dash, a joystick game that didn't work on a phone. I threw the joystick out and rebuilt it around one action: read a jelly's color code, then cast that sequence on four elemental pads with a tap or a swipe. Read, cast, survive.

Color is never the only signal. Every element pairs with a glyph (flame, drop, star, leaf), and an Okabe-Ito color-safe palette sits next to the standard one. A reduced-flashing mode tones down the PRISM bursts.

The jellies, nebula, barrier, and shield are canvas shaders, not sprite sheets, so the game stays small and sharp at any size. Around the five-wave campaign there is an Endless Rift, seeded runs, difficulty tiers, coins, and a permanent Prism Shop.

It runs in Godot 4.6 at 720×1280 portrait. More than 150 checks, headless and rendered, gate every build. A tester APK is built; feel-testing on physical phones comes next.
