MAO Bay: Field & Office is a working fictional-data demonstration of municipal agriculture and fisheries monitoring: people, seasonal activities, field visits, scoped supervisor review, and immutable period report snapshots. Every record, coordinate, and staff account in the demo is synthetic so the workflow can be tested openly without exposing real personal data.

## Role separation and immutable period snapshots

Five distinct roles keep field collection separate from report approval: two field officers manage their own assigned activities, a reviewer audits submitted visits and approves narratives, a viewer reads approved totals and issued reports, and an administrator manages registries without approval authority.

Local persistence runs through transactional SQLite so restarts retain every visit revision and issued report snapshot, paired with Supabase wiring and backup/restore scripts for deployment.

## Paper-clear GIS boundaries

The mapping view pairs stored WGS84 polygons with Turf.js spherical area estimates, flagging whenever a reported farm area diverges from mapped geometry by more than 5% or 0.01 hectares. Operators can inspect boundary revisions and export approved polygons directly to GeoJSON, KML, or KMZ.
