Needs vs. Wants asks one deliberate question before a purchase becomes permanent: is it a Need or a Want? That pause is the core mechanic. Now live on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.needsvswants.app), it helps students and young earners track everyday peso spending against a realistic daily budget without turning pocket money into accounting software.

I designed and built the native Android app in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose around a physical ledger-and-notepad aesthetic: green Need seals, crimson Want stamps, warm paper surfaces, and crisp tabular totals.

## Offline-first ledger and on-phone advisor

Local entries live in a Room database so logging a jeepney fare, coffee, or meal works immediately with zero network dependency or mandatory sign-in. Optional Supabase backup and restore let users carry history cleanly across devices.

Recent Google Play releases (through v2.0.56 / build 64) rolled out a v3 ledger UI with whole-app motion choreography, a Spending Patterns breakdown tab, a folio paywall, and an on-phone budget advisor that checks whether an impulse buy fits today's remaining room before you tap save.

Download Needs vs. Wants on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.needsvswants.app) or test the interactive walkthrough at [needs-vs-wants.vercel.app](https://needs-vs-wants.vercel.app).
