Monte Carlo de Laguna is an independent resort website concept built for client review in Calauan, Laguna. Instead of a loud resort flyer packed with flashing badges, the design pairs warm cream paper, deep forest green, serif headlines, and sunset pool photography to make the property feel unhurried and clear.

## Editorial rhythm and zero-friction client handoff

The page walks prospective guests through the grounds in alternating editorial bands: an arched sunset hero with a quick inquiry composer, room and garden highlights, a three-card pace section (poolside, gardens, gatherings), an events showcase on a deep forest band, a photo gallery, location map notes, and an expandable FAQ.

Because non-technical resort owners often review drafts straight from Messenger or email downloads, I packaged two editions alongside `index.html`: a modular web folder with responsive 480w/960w/1600w WebP imagery, and a single self-contained `monte-carlo-laguna.html` file embedding all photos, fonts, CSS, and scripts so it opens offline with zero external requests.
