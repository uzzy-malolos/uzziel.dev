Long Way Around is my second Godot RPG - smaller scope than Heart and Soil, more narrative weight than TIRADOR. It runs at 384x216 internal resolution on 16x16 tiles, built in Godot 4.6 with an explicit license trail in `ASSETS.md` and `CREDITS.md` for every third-party pack.

After finishing the four-act story and three endings, October passes rebuilt the presentation and feel end to end: a 21-character paper-doll sprite generator (`tools/gen_npc_sprites.gd`) with walk cycles and 3x sprite-cropped portraits so world bodies match dialogue faces, wandering NPCs with flag-aware floating barks, smoothed camera follow, a persistent World HUD, a 14-track OST paired with JDWasabi SFX, and floating battle damage numbers.

A 21-suite headless and release-pack verification gate (`tools/ship.ps1`) checks every act, battle feedback, NPC wandering, shop reachability, audit regressions, and desktop export packaging before builds go out.
