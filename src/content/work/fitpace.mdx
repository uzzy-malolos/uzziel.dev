Fitness Pace brings the parts of a training week into one place: decide what to train, record every set or meal, and feed that history back into the next session. Now live on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.fitpace.app), the app grew out of PeerFit into a complete mobile companion alongside the [Fitness Pace website](https://fitpace-pearl.vercel.app).

Workouts, nutrition, daily steps, and coaching conversations share the same underlying state rather than living across disconnected calculators.

## Guided training without rigid templates

Training starts with explicit choices around available equipment, target schedule, and intensity. Users pick preset routines or build custom workouts, track active sessions set by set, inspect full workout history, and follow 90-day lift trends as baseline capacity climbs.

Nutrition logging sits beside training rather than behind a separate paywall screen. Users log meals via itemized photo estimation, barcode scan, nutrition label scan, or manual entries, keeping protein and calorie balance visible alongside workout recovery.

## Clear progress and adaptive PACE coaching

The progress dashboard brings weight trends, activity streaks, weekly adherence, and lift charts onto one calm overview built around realistic 7-day, 30-day, and 90-day windows. An optional coaching companion called PACE answers questions using recent workouts and logged intake as real context.

On the engineering side, I built the mobile app with Expo and React Native, backed by a TypeScript Fastify API, PostgreSQL persistence, and Railway deployment. Download Fitness Pace directly on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.fitpace.app) or explore the interactive planner at [fitpace-pearl.vercel.app](https://fitpace-pearl.vercel.app).
