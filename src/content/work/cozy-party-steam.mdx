Cozy Party: Steam Edition rebuilds the [browser edition](/work/cozy-party-game) from scratch for PC on Unity 6 (`6000.6.9f1`, URP 17, Linear ACES). Every character runs on an active-ragdoll 14-bone plushie rig wrapped in a 14-shell volumetric fur shader with multi-light combed sheen, blinking eyelids, KO stars, and grounded contact shadows.

## Six playable scenes in one suite

1. **Clubhouse Lobby:** Interactive 3D warm-up room with a kickable soccer ball, live plush tint preview, map and rules selector, career stats, and Steam lobby hook.
2. **Board Party:** 24-tile branching 3D island across Emerald Meadow (Windmill Gale), Sunken Atoll (High Tide), and Starry Summit (Meteor Strikes), complete with 30 HP Pummel items, Item Shop, 3D dice, golden Trophies, and automatic minigame rotation.
3. **Ice Sumo:** Slippery ice floe brawler with Giant Mallet, Frying Pan, Bomb, Freeze Ray, and Banana Peel pickups.
4. **Plush Soccer:** 2v2 active-ragdoll pitch with a bouncy physics ball, 60-second clock, first-to-3 goals, and striker bots.
5. **Crumble Drop:** Two-tier 74-tile falling hexagon survival arena.
6. **Podium Finale:** 6.5-second playable victory brawl ending in a framed Polaroid snapshot.

## Physics, fur shading, and tactile Paper & Sticker UI

Recent passes tuned non-alloc active-ragdoll gait blending and counter-steering, added URP bloom and depth of field, painted procedural PBR surface textures (wood planks, stone cliffs, rug weave, roof shingles), and introduced a full Paper & Sticker UI theme with gamepad navigation.
