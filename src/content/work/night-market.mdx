Night Market is a cooperative cooking game with a horror shift. Players run the stalls together: cook, serve, keep orders moving, and fix the breaker when the lights go out. Then the Watcher shows up.

I kept the co-op loop fair under pressure. The Watcher is harmless until it catches you, and spaced Blackout and Rush warnings give players time to react. A server-checked shelter in the back kitchen is the safe spot. A catch costs health and the food you were carrying, but banked earnings stay safe, so a scare never wipes a good shift.

Every asset is my own Blender work: kitchen gear, food, the cast, roofs, lanterns, drainage, wet paving, and plants. They share a few small PBR textures so phones can run the scene.

The build runs in Studio and passes its gameplay checks: cooking and serving, breaker repair, normal and reduced scares, and avatar recovery. A human playtest for scare timing and difficulty comes before any publishing.
