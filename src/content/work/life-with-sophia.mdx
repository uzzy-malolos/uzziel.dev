Life with Sophia is a content and product-curation site built in Next.js 16 and MDX for sharing everyday finds - gaming peripherals, desk tools, and youth camp essentials - paired with short stories that outgrow a social caption.

## Structured finds and MDX storytelling

Rather than forcing product picks into a generic link-in-bio grid, the site organizes curated items with category filters, transparent `rel="sponsored nofollow"` affiliate disclosures on every outbound button, and a dedicated Disclosure page explaining the AI-created character upfront.

Longer narratives live in typed MDX story files compiled directly through `@next/mdx`, rendered beside related finds with Vercel Analytics wired for clean readership metrics and zero heavy tracking scripts.

Visit the live site at [life-with-sophia.vercel.app](https://life-with-sophia.vercel.app).
