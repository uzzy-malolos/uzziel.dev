Comtus AI turns your PDFs, documents, and notes into a study partner that keeps every notebook on your device. Ask anything and it answers from your own material with the exact page it came from, turns missed quiz items into flashcards, and maps out a review schedule before exam day.

I designed and built the React and TypeScript application, its Tauri desktop wrapper (Windows `.exe` and macOS `.dmg` with signed auto-updates via `comtus-releases`), and the surrounding product site. The interface borrows from a working study desk: warm paper, dark ink, a brass accent, generous reading type, and resizable keyboard-operable panes.

## From material to practice

A notebook holds source documents, notes, timelines, and saved study work together. Users pick which sources enter a conversation, ask cited questions, and generate flashcards, quizzes, summaries, or guides while mathematical notation and coordinate graphs carry cleanly into exported files.

An optional companion pane called Compad shares the exact conversation and draft state as the full pane, so switching between compact reading and deep study never breaks flow.

## Shipped on web and desktop

[Comtus AI is live on the web](https://www.comtus-ai.xyz) with zero account wall, alongside the v0.3.9 desktop installers for Windows and macOS. Theme switching, reading size controls, reduced motion, dialog focus management, and recovery after deleting saved work all ship as part of the core experience.
