Rimegard is a PC-first 3D isometric RPG set after the Great Whiteout of 1889, when deep crust drilling ruptured a subterranean cryo-seal and froze the surface world. Survivors cluster inside dormant volcanic craters around city-sized Titan Boilers, stepping beyond the airlocks into Frost-Moss Flats and Sub-Zero Culvert B1 to harvest Heart-Cinders before the furnace pressure drops.

## Classic grind meets thermal survival

Combat combines keyboard-and-mouse click-to-move action, job changes, hireable mercenaries, and supply management with a persistent Body Heat system. Venture too far from brazier camps or steam vents and the cold narrows your options; light a temporary camp, clear frozen culverts, and pull enemy packs carefully before ice reclaims the ground.

## Built in Godot 4.6 and Blender for PC

The playable offline story slice runs on Godot 4.6.3 (Compatibility renderer) with 14 Blender-rigged 3D actors, scripted terrain and masonry generators (`tools/blender/build_environment.py`, `tools/build_mob_surfaces.py`), and an authoritative local host handling zone transfers, inventory, party state, and saves.
