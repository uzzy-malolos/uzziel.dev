These are two-minute vertical films for the Bible Adventures channel, made in a soft storybook style: a muted palette of ochre, slate, sage, and cream, black ink outlines that boil like hand-drawn lines, paper grain, round-headed figures, and orange woolly lambs.

There are no keyframes drawn by hand. A shared engine reads the narrator's word timings and drives the camera, characters, scenery, transitions, and captions from them. When a line is spoken, the scene that goes with it plays on that word.

Each new story adds to the shared rigs. Abraham and Isaac brought talking mouths, hugs, kneeling, a donkey, a ram, and a page-turn transition. Adam and Eve added the serpent. Every film renders with a branded intro and outro, an original score, and a mix at -14 LUFS.

Four films are finished. The series continues one story at a time.
