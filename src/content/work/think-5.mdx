Think 5 boils party trivia down to one clean loop: read a prompt like "Name 5 countries in Asia," pick a difficulty-locked clock (1 minute, 1.5 minutes, or 2 minutes), and type five valid answers before the bar hits zero. No accounts, no ads, and no internet connection needed.

## Offline database, 5 Streak, and Filipino support

Built natively in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, Think 5 validates entries locally against its bundled question and alias dataset, blocks duplicate answers immediately, offers point-deducting hints, and supports both English and Filipino interfaces in light and dark themes.

Alongside classic 5 Rush rounds, players can push a survival run in **5 Streak** (five out of five to stay alive), try the shared **Daily Challenge**, or run local **Party Mode** passes across phones. Keyboard-open landscape layouts, 200% enlarged-font accessibility scaling, pause guards, and JVM unit tests were verified directly on device.
