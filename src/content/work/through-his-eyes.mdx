Through His Eyes is a vertical (`1080x1920`, 24fps) cinematic short-film series telling New Testament healing accounts from a tight, off-axis third-person perspective. Three finished episodes lead the series: *The Blind Man Healed*, *12 Years of Sickness*, and *The Touch of Faith*.

## Code-driven editorial assembly and sound design

Rather than manually dragging dozens of clips on a timeline for every revision, each episode compiles from a versioned Python and FFmpeg edit script (`build_edit.py`) paired with timed `.srt` subtitles and a strict three-track audio blueprint:

- **Narrator voice-over** placed cleanly inside open beats so narration never collides with on-screen character lines.
- **Synced character dialogue and crowd ambience** that duck dynamically under speech, including low-pass silence drops at dramatic turning points.
- **Continuous original instrumental score** mixed underneath narration and swelling during quiet visual beats, graded with a consistent warm 35mm film palette across clips.
