What If is a series of short films that each take one question seriously. What if Atlantis rose to the surface? What if Tesla's tower had been funded? Each episode runs two to three minutes, with narration, a score, and data callouts where real numbers help.

Every frame is a real-time 3D scene in three.js, rendered frame by frame in headless Chrome on the GPU. The picture is a pure function of time, so I can render any moment on demand, re-render one shot after a fix, and resume a long render in 10-second chunks. Each film comes out at 1920×1080 and 60 fps, mastered to -14 LUFS.

Episode 2 states up front that the "Tesla called the pyramid a power plant" story has no source. Then it plays the what-if straight: Wardenclyffe in 1901, Morgan stops paying, the funded tower fires, New York in 1931 without wires. It ends on the real 1917 demolition and the wireless charging in your phone today.
