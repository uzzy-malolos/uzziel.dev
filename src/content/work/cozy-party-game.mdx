Cozy Party is the game you open when friends are ready to play right away: articulated 3D plush kin racing across an island board and competing across 22 minigames, straight from a browser tab or standalone Windows desktop EXE. No accounts and no friction to start a match.

## 3D board, walkable lobby, and 22 minigames

The latest visual and physics passes rebuilt character rendering and presentation end to end: physical velvet plush sheen with fuzz shells, glossy eyes and noses, procedural PBR surface maps, 2048px soft shadows, full-body jump and locomotion articulation, and a custom clubhouse prop kit.

## Online WebRTC rooms, desktop EXE, and Steam edition

Up to 8 players share a single keyboard on couch night, plug in gamepads, or scan a lobby QR code from a phone over WebRTC peer channels backed by a dedicated Singapore Railway signaling server and Cloudflare TURN relay credentials.
