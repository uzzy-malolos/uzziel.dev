Cozy Party is the game you open when friends are ready to play right away: rigged 3D animal kin racing across an island board and competing across 19 minigames, straight from a browser tab. No install, no accounts, and no build step required for players.

## 3D board, walkable lobby, and 19 minigames

Originally prototyped across a hybrid 2D canvas and three.js renderer, the game now runs in a unified 3D three.js world with a walkable 3D lobby, rigged Blender animal characters with ragdoll physics, wind-swayed scenery landmarks, host party stakes, and dynamic camera follow.

The roster spans 19 minigames with fast input coalescing, eased mood animations, and mid-match seat rejoin if someone drops on a spotty connection. Up to 8 players share a single keyboard on couch night, plug in standard controllers, or scan a room code from a phone over WebRTC peer data channels.

Play immediately in any browser at [cozy-party-indol.vercel.app](https://cozy-party-indol.vercel.app).
